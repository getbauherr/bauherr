## Problem {#problem}
<!-- What is wrong or wanted, in the requester's words. One short paragraph, no solution. A bug: what happens, and steps to reproduce when known. A feature or change: who needs it and why. -->

## Context {#context}
<!-- Where, who, since when, how often. Pages, screens, accounts, versions. A bug: where it happens. -->

## Acceptance criteria {#acceptance-criteria}
<!-- At least one checkable statement of what "done" looks like, one bullet each (a bug: what should happen instead). Triage and the plan are checked against them. -->

## Type and severity {#type-severity}
<!-- bug, feature, change or question; for a bug low, medium or high. On the forge this is the label bauherr:type:<kind>. -->

## Urgent {#urgent}
<!-- Only for an urgent bug: what is broken, for whom, since when. Written by the requester; the Bauherr decides on the fast lane. -->

## Follow-up {#follow-up}
<!-- Only when the request reports a problem with an earlier one: "Follow-up to #<number>". -->

## Links {#links}
<!-- Related tickets, pages, documents. -->

## Open {#open}
<!-- What is still unknown or missing, one bullet each. -->
