# Requests

A request is a wish written as a conversation. The factory asks what it needs and turns the answer into a ticket.

## Who can ask

- **Bauherren and Admins**: Requests is in the sidebar. The title carries a Product switcher. The list and the new request belong to the chosen Product. The last choice is remembered.
- **Product owners**: see only the Products they own. With one Product there is no switcher. Requests is their whole shell.

A new request starts in the chosen Product. You can change the Product until you send the first message. After that it is fixed, and a draft keeps its own Product.

## What the ticket says

- The issue body says "Requested by the Bauherr" or "Requested by a product owner".
- The story shows the name with the role, for example "Mara (Bauherr)".
- A request from a Bauherr is not an outside reporter. Its Brief is still decided by the usual rules.
- The factory's messages show its own avatar and name.

Open `/requests/<id>` to see whose turn it is.
