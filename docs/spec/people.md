# People: roles and assignments

One auth collection, `bauherren`, holds every account. `role` is `admin`, `bauherr` or `product-owner`. Rank: Admin > Bauherr > Product owner (`lib/people.ts`: `rank`, `isBauherr` = Admin or Bauherr, `isProductOwner`). Only an Admin sets or changes a role. The first account becomes Admin; the last Admin cannot be demoted or removed; nobody changes their own role. Only an Admin deletes (archives) or restores a Workspace (`archiveWorkspace`, `restoreWorkspace` in `lib/workspace-delete.ts`; any Admin may restore, 14 days); the last live Workspace cannot be deleted.

## Assignment

A Product owner has `assignments: [{ workspace, scope: 'workspace' | 'products', products: [] }]`. `scope` is explicit: an empty `products` list under `products` grants nothing, never "all". At most one assignment per Workspace; its Products must belong to that Workspace. A hook keeps the tenant plugin's `tenants` equal to the Workspaces of the assignments (whatever a caller or an SSO sync sends). Bauherren keep `tenants` as before and have no assignments (changing a Product owner into a Bauherr clears them; the other way starts with none). Deleting a Product removes it from assignments. `assignmentOf(user, workspace)` and `canSeeProduct(user, product)` read this; a Bauherr or Admin sees every Product of their Workspaces.

## What a Product owner sees

Everything below is filtered by the assignment in one module, `lib/people.ts`: `assignmentOf`, `canSeeProduct`, `productScope` / `productsVisibleTo` / `requestsVisibleTo` (the `where` of every list) and `mediaVisibleTo`. A Bauherr or Admin sees what they saw before. Anything outside the assignment (Product B of the same Workspace, another Workspace, a missing id) answers the same 404, never a 403.

- **Home** is Requests (`homeOf`). The sidebar has the Inbox (with the count of what asks them), Requests (with the draft count) and Profile; the user menu has Sign out; the header has the sidebar trigger, the breadcrumb and New request. No dock, no chat button. No Workspace switcher, command palette, presence, Release notes or Settings. A Product owner works in the first Workspace they are assigned in.
- **Requests** (`/requests`): the four sections (Needs you, Drafts, In the factory, Done), limited to the sent requests of assigned Products plus their own drafts. "Needs you" holds only the requester's steps on their own request, shown as status text ("Waiting for your answer · waits for you") with no button: a checkpoint addressed to them is decided in the Inbox or on the Ticket page (see "Addressed checkpoints"). No By column, no drag to order (the order of the board stays the Bauherr's). They write requests in the request conversation (`/requests/new` opens their empty draft for an assigned Product, `/requests/<id>` continues it, Discard asks first): the same shell as a Bauherr's, with the model as a plain label and no cost chip. Report a problem is not offered. Their drafts are listed and continued there; the request page's card is read-only, its images shown through the signed-in route. An urgent request of a Product owner proposes the fast lane (`bauherr:expedite-proposed`); only an Admin's goes straight onto it (`canPutOnFastLane`).
- **Request** (`/requests/[id]`) and **Ticket** (`/tickets/[id]`, a reduced page): the request card, the plain-word path and timeline, "waits for", proof for review (the `artifacts` of the Ticket through `/api/tickets/[id]/artifacts/[artifactId]`), the release note of a released Ticket, the comment stream and "Comment on #n". Not shown: Runs, steps, worklog, cost, tokens, models, executors or agent names, the plan and its decisions, budgets, settings, the workflow editor, Insights, the Agent team, the Bauherr's Inbox, links to the forge issue. The data is read by `loadOwnerTicket` / `loadRequest`, which read no Runs.
- **Comments**: the stream lists the forge comments of the Ticket without the factory's own (markers, escalations); images are shown through the signed-in route. Writing a comment arrives with the conversation panel of the Ticket page; there is no question card and no decision on the reduced page.
- **Media**: files of a request (screenshots) and of comments of an assigned Product are served by `/intake/api/media/[id]` to a signed-in Product owner when `mediaVisibleTo` holds (an open draft's files only to its author); anything else is a 404. Review artifacts are never behind a public link. The signed public link `/m/<id>/<sig>` is still handed out only for images that end up in forge text (request screenshots, comment images) ; no page of a Product owner links to it.
- **Docked chat**: closed. `chatWho` refuses a Product owner, so every `/api/chat/*` route (context, media, threads and its sub-routes: confirm, feedback, messages, run) answers 403, and no chat entry point is rendered.
- **Server actions** open to them: sign-in and sign-out, and the request conversation's own (`requestState`, `requestChange`, `requestSubmit`, `discardIntake`; each answers only for their own request of an assigned Product). Report a problem and `postDockComment` stay closed. Routes open to them: `POST /api/requests/<id>/turn` (404 for any request that is not theirs) and `POST /api/requests/media`; every `/api/chat/*` route stays closed.
- **API**: the artifact route and the media route as above; no Payload REST collection except their own account.

Not for a Product owner yet: commenting from the Ticket page, forge comments counting as decisions (slice 4). A decision is theirs only where a checkpoint is addressed to them (below).

## Addressed checkpoints

Every human checkpoint asks one of two people: the **Bauherr** or the **Product owner**. A Bauherr or Admin can always decide what asks the Product owner (and is recorded as `bauherr`); the reverse never holds.

- **Factory file** (`docs/spec/workflow.md` §2). `gate: human` means `{by: bauherr}`; `gate: {by: bauherr | product-owner}` on `plan` and `release`, `production_gate` the same, `refine: {by: ...}` on the `triage` stage for Triage's questions, `by:` on a `human` stage. A file or stored version without `by:` asks the Bauherr everywhere. `lint` refuses anything else (`gate` with other keys, `refine` off the triage stage, `by` off a human stage). Compiled, only `product-owner` is stored (`gate_by`, `production_gate_by`, `refine_by`, `by`), so a file that does not use it compiles to what it did before and applies as no new version.
- **Shipped default** (`pack/factory.yaml`, the Standard preset): refinement `product-owner`, plan approval `bauherr`, production `bauherr`, acceptance on staging `product-owner` *per Product* (below). Careful asks the Bauherr for acceptance on staging, Solo asks the Bauherr everywhere. An existing Workspace with an applied version keeps its checkpoints until it applies a new version (the shipped file or a preset gives it the default). One without an applied version runs the shipped default, which asks the Product owner only where a Product has one assigned (next point), so nothing changes for it until a Product owner is assigned.
- **No Product owner assigned**: a checkpoint addressed to the Product owner of a Product without one asks the Bauherr (`askedOf`, `lib/addressee.ts`). Acceptance on staging is the one checkpoint that does not fall back: with `gate: {by: product-owner}` on `release` it is on only for a Product with an assigned Product owner and the Product switch **Product owner tests on staging** (`products.productOwnerTestsStaging`, Product > Approvals) on (`stagingGateOn`, `lib/mode.ts`); otherwise the Product has no acceptance checkpoint and passes staging by itself, as without a gate (`watch-release`). `gate: human` (the Bauherr) is on for every Product, whatever the switch says.
- **`mayDecide(user, {ticket, addressee})`** (`lib/people.ts`): a Bauherr or Admin of the Ticket's Workspace always; a Product owner only when the checkpoint (or the decision row) is addressed to the Product owner AND the Ticket's Product is in their assignment; anyone else never. Every gate function in `lib/actions.ts` starts with `loadGate`, which loads the Ticket and asks it: `approvePlan` (plan), `acceptStaging` and `requestChanges` (staging), `decide` (the `human` stage), `approveProduction` (production), `postDecisions` and `finishRefinement` (refine, or plan for the sheet at the plan gate). The UI actions, the MCP human tools (`approve`, `accept_staging`, `decide`, `approve_production`, `request_changes`) and the chat tools call these same functions, so there is one rule. A Ticket outside the assignment answers `NotInWorkspace` (404); one they see that is not addressed to them answers `NotAddressed` ("this decision is not addressed to you"). An agent key is refused on every human tool (`callTool`, `access: 'human'`) whatever the checkpoint says; a Product owner has no MCP at all.
- **`tickets.needsBy`** (`bauherr | product-owner`) is written by the gate sweep together with `needsYou` (`needBy` from the file, then `askedOf`); null while nothing is asked. The Inbox, the sidebar badge and the forge notice follow it: a Product owner's Inbox and badge count only Tickets with `needsBy = product-owner` on their Products; a Bauherr's Inbox lists every card and marks those that ask the Product owner ("Asked: Product owner", `data-testid="asked"`). The forge notice (assign and @mention) goes to the `githubLogin` of the assigned Product owners when it asks them, else to the Product's gate owners. The migration backfills `needsBy = bauherr` for every open need. Rows that name the Product owner (`for: product-owner`) on a checkpoint that asks the Bauherr are counted when the sheet is written or answered (`tickets.openOwnerRows`, `storeOpenCritical`); with the need Answer questions or Answer decisions they also put the Ticket in a Product owner's Inbox and badge (`asksOwner`, `lib/addressee.ts`), still without a forge call at render. The typed turn route (`hasRows`) uses the same rule as the other refine actions: rows addressed to the person.
- **Decision rows**: a row of a plan or Triage sheet may carry `for: product-owner | bauherr` (canonical `critical: true. for: bauherr.`, or a sixth table column); without it the row asks whom the checkpoint asks. A sheet can be mixed: the refinement conversation of a Product owner (`RefineConversation`, reached from their Inbox card or `/tickets/<id>?view=conversation`; the turn route `/api/refine/[ticket]/turn` and the actions `refineState`, `refineSay`, `refinePick`, `refineExplain`, `refineRest`, `refineChange`, `refineFinish` are open to them) lists only their rows (`Point.for`), the model sees only those, and `postDecisions` refuses an answer, a reopen or a new row for someone else's. A Bauherr sees and answers all rows. "Send to planning" stays refused while any critical row is open, whoever it is for. A Product owner sees no model choice and no cost (`View.viewer`: `models` and `cost` are empty, usage and the session's model are stripped); `refineTier` stays the Bauherr's. `decideAction` (an Inbox card) is open to them for `approvePlan`, `requestChanges`, `acceptStaging`, `approveProduction` and `decide` only.
- **The touch** (`human_touches`) records `actor` (who decided), `as` (`bauherr | product-owner`: how they decided) and `addressee` (whom the checkpoint asked): a Bauherr who accepts a Product owner's staging is `actor: <their email>, as: bauherr, addressee: product-owner`.
- **Workflow editor** (`/workflow/edit`): every checkpoint row has an "Asked" select (Bauherr / Product owner; `Asked.tsx`, in the step panel for the triage, plan, release and `human` steps) written by `setAsked` as `{by: product-owner}` or back to the plain forms; the strip on top groups the points by who is asked ("You are asked at n points" / "The Product owner is asked at n points"); Review changes says who is asked now (`describeChanges`).

## Closed by default

Outside what is listed under "What a Product owner sees", a Product owner reaches nothing except `/login`, sign-out, the OIDC flow, `/settings/profile` (`/settings` redirects there), `/api/health`, the public signed image link `/m/<id>/<sig>`, and their own account over Payload REST (read; update of `password` and `uiPrefs` only: any other changed field is refused by the account's `beforeValidate` hook, no API key can be enabled for them).

- `requireBauherr` answers a Product owner with a 404 and `getBauherr` returns null, so every page, server action and route built on them is closed without a check at the call site. `getPerson` / `requirePerson` are for the few places open to both.
- The intake `actor` (the widget API), `/mcp` (also behind an agent key) and `callTool` refuse a Product owner. `chatWho` refuses them too (the dock is closed to a Product owner).
- Every collection has an explicit `access`: `col()` defaults every operation to `bauherrOnly` (Admin or Bauherr; the multi-tenant plugin still narrows to their Workspaces), a collection overrides single operations. Payload's own `payload-*` collections are closed the same way in `payload.config.ts` (`closeBuiltIns`), except `payload-preferences`, which Payload keys to the user. A Workspace member can no longer write any collection a Product owner could not; Bauherren and Admins lose nothing the app used.
- OIDC: `OIDC_PRODUCT_OWNER_ROLE` maps a role claim value to `product-owner` (the admin claim wins; unset = nobody is mapped). A new SSO Product owner has no assignment and sees nothing. A re-sync never raises a Product owner to `bauherr` (`resyncRole`); assignments are never touched by SSO. `OIDC_BAUHERR_ROLE` makes a mapped role mandatory for sign-in. See `docs/auth.md`.

## The guard test and how to open something

`apps/server/tests/product-owner-guard.test.ts` puts every page, route handler, server action, chat tool and collection into exactly one of two lists: `BAUHERR_*` (a Product owner is refused) and `PO_*` (open to a Product owner, each with its reason). The sets are globbed from the source, so a new page, route, action, chat tool or collection fails the partition test until someone decides. Everything in a `BAUHERR_*` list is exercised as a Product owner (pages rendered, actions called, routes fetched with their session, collections per operation, MCP tools): it must answer 404 / 401 / 403 / refuse. Everything in a `PO_*` list that shows or changes data is exercised against Product B of the same Workspace, another Workspace and a colleague's draft, and must answer 404 (the last describe of the file); `tests/product-owner-view.test.ts` covers what a Product owner does see. To open something to a Product owner: change its rule (use `requirePerson` / `currentPersonWorkspace` / a narrower check, never loosen `requireBauherr` or `acting`), filter its data in `lib/people.ts`, move it to the `PO_*` list with the reason, add its Product B check, and update this page.

## Forge channel

A gate label or an answers comment (`<!-- bauherr:decisions -->`) written on the forge counts as a decision only when its author's forge login is linked to a person who may decide it. The account's `githubLogin` holds the login (a GitLab username too; the field is the one linked forge login). `lib/forge-channel.ts` is the one place that decides.

- **Who counts.** A Bauherr or Admin of the Ticket's Workspace (Admins of every Workspace), always; a Product owner only when assigned to the Ticket's Product AND the checkpoint (for answers: the decision row, `for:` else the checkpoint's `by`) asks the Product owner (`askedFor`: a checkpoint for the Product owner of a Product without one asks the Bauherr). A Product owner of Product A has no say on Product B. The Expedite lane is the Bauherr's. Logins match case-insensitively; one login belongs to one account (case ignored, refused on save).
- **Labels** (`lib/gate-labels.ts`): the actor of the latest `labeled` event is looked up as above; anyone else's label is removed, with one `label-rejected` comment (marker per label and time) naming the reason. The factory's own credential still needs the recorded touch, as before.
- **Answers comments**: `decisionComments` is the one read of a Ticket's comments for decisions; every page, sweep and action goes through it. The untrusted answers comments are left out, so `answersOf`, `openCritical` and the pages see only what counts. A Product owner's comment keeps only the answers and added rows that ask the Product owner. The sweeps and the poller (`notify`) post one `answers-ignored` comment per ignored comment (marker keyed on the comment id), saying why; a page render never writes.
- **The factory's own comments** (the Ticket page, chat and MCP all write the answers through `postDecisions`) are recognised by the forge's author field against the credential's login (`whoami`, cached 5 minutes per Connection), or a `[bot]` author for an App token; never by comment text. The factory edits only its own comment in place; where the newest counted comment is a person's, it posts the merged answers as a newer comment. Residual: an agent session running under the factory's own credential is the factory to this check (as for labels, see workflow.md).
- **`products.gateOwners` is gone.** Who is assigned and mentioned at a gate is the derived set: the linked logins of the Workspace's Bauherren and Admins (and, where a checkpoint asks the Product owner, the assigned Product owners). The migration `20261112_170000_forge_channel` keeps logins that match an account (the account already holds them) and moves the others to `products.unlinkedGateOwners`.
- **Settings › Health** lists, per Product, the old logins without an account ("link the login on the person's profile") until they match one, and says plainly when a Workspace with a forge has no Bauherr or Admin with a linked login (decisions by label or comment then count for nobody; the Ticket page still works).
- **Linking.** The profile edits one's own login, a Product owner's too (`saveOwnLogin`; the only account field a Product owner may change besides password and preferences); an Admin edits anyone's under People, which lists who has none.
